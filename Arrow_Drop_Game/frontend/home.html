HOW TO PLAY

  • Use the arrow keys on your keyboard to match the direction of the falling arrows
  • Press the correct key before the arrow reaches the bottom of the screen to score points
  • You will have three lives at the start.
  • Be careful! Missing an arrow will cost you a life
  • As you level up, the arrows will fall faster and faster!
  • The game ends once you've lost all your lives

Press any key to continue!
